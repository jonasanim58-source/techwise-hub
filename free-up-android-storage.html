How to Free Up Storage on Android

Safe ways to reclaim storage space without accidentally deleting important files.

Check what is using space

Open your phone's storage settings and identify the largest categories first. Videos, downloads, offline media and app caches are often worth checking.

Remove unnecessary downloads

Review your Downloads folder and delete files you no longer need. If something is important, back it up first.

Review large videos

Large videos can consume much more space than photos. Move important files to a trusted backup location before deleting local copies.

Clear cache carefully

Some apps let you clear temporary cached data. Cache is different from app data: clearing app data can sign you out or remove local settings, so read the option carefully.

Uninstall unused apps

If you have not used an app for months, consider removing it. You can reinstall legitimate apps later when needed.

Keep a backup

Before a major cleanup, make sure irreplaceable photos, documents and contacts have a backup. Storage cleanup should never start with deleting files you cannot replace.
