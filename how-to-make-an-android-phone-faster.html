How to Make an Android Phone Faster

If your Android phone has become slow or keeps freezing, there are several simple things you can try before replacing it.

1. Restart your phone

A simple restart can clear temporary processes and give your phone a fresh start. If your phone has been running for a long time, restart it and check whether performance improves.

2. Free up storage space

A phone that is almost full can become harder to manage and may have less room for temporary files and updates. Remove apps you no longer use, delete unnecessary downloads, and move important photos or videos to another storage location.

For more storage tips, see our guide on how to free up storage on Android .

3. Remove apps you don't use

Unused apps take up storage and some may continue running background processes. Review your installed apps and uninstall those you no longer need.

4. Update Android and your apps

Check for available system and app updates. Updates can include bug fixes, security improvements, and performance changes.

Only install updates from trusted sources such as your phone's built-in system updater or official app store.

5. Check which apps use the most resources

Android phones usually provide battery, storage, or app-management screens that can help you identify applications using significant resources. If one app consistently causes problems, check whether it needs an update or whether you still need it.

6. Clear unnecessary app data carefully

Some apps accumulate temporary files over time. Android may provide options to clear an app's cache. Be careful when using options that clear all app data because this can remove locally stored settings or sign you out.

7. Reduce unnecessary background activity

Review apps that frequently run in the background. Restricting background activity can sometimes help battery life and responsiveness, although the exact settings depend on your Android version and phone manufacturer.

8. Avoid suspicious apps

Apps from unknown or untrusted sources can create security and performance problems. Remove applications that you do not recognize or no longer trust.

You can also read our guide on how to spot a suspicious app .

9. Don't expect every old phone to become fast

Software maintenance can help, but hardware also matters. Older phones may have limited memory, slower processors, or aging batteries. If you have tried the basic improvements and the phone remains consistently slow, upgrading may eventually be the better option.

Quick checklist

  • Restart the phone.
  • Free up unnecessary storage.
  • Remove unused apps.
  • Install available system and app updates.
  • Check which apps consume significant resources.
  • Clear unnecessary cached data carefully.
  • Review background activity.
  • Remove suspicious applications.

Final thoughts

A slow Android phone does not always need to be replaced immediately. Start with the simple steps above, monitor the results, and keep your important files backed up before making major changes.
