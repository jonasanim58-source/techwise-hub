Best Free Tools for Students

A practical starter list for notes, files, documents and studying.

Cloud documents

Use a reputable cloud document service to keep notes accessible across devices and reduce the risk of losing a single local copy.

Task management

A simple task list can be enough. Organize work by deadline and break large assignments into smaller actions.

PDF tools

Use trustworthy PDF viewers and converters. Be careful with websites that ask you to upload confidential school or personal documents.

File backups

Keep important assignments in at least two locations. A cloud copy plus an offline copy is a useful starting point.

Focus tools

A timer and a distraction-free workspace can make study sessions more manageable. Try short focused sessions followed by planned breaks.

Privacy

Before using any free online tool, check what data it collects and avoid uploading documents containing sensitive information unless you trust the provider.
