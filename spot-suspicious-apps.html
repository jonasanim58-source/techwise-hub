How to Spot a Suspicious App

Practical checks before installing an unfamiliar Android app.

Use official sources

Prefer the official app store or the developer's verified distribution channel. Avoid random download pages offering modified or cracked versions.

Check the developer

Look at the developer name, other apps, support information and whether the developer has a credible online presence.

Review permissions

Ask whether the requested permissions make sense. A simple flashlight app requesting access to unrelated sensitive information deserves extra scrutiny.

Read reviews carefully

Look for patterns rather than one angry or enthusiastic review. Sudden waves of generic reviews can be a warning sign.

Keep your device updated

Security updates and app updates can fix vulnerabilities. Use a screen lock and enable the security features provided by your device.

When in doubt, don't install

If an app feels suspicious, stop. Search for a reputable alternative rather than taking a risk with personal information.
